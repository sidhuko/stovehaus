## What stock may and may not do here

Two rules, enforced by judgement rather than by the audit script, because they
are about meaning rather than markup:

1. **Atmosphere and process only.** Steam, cedar, fire, woodland, a press brake,
   sparks off a grinder. A stock photograph of another manufacturer's heater
   presented as a Versa is misrepresentation — a dealer would spot it, and it
   would undo the one thing this site is built to protect.
2. **Never beside a figure as if it were the product.** A photograph next to a
   spec reads as that spec's subject. The Versa pages carry a löyly shot whose
   crop holds on stones, steam and water — material, not an identifiable unit.

`sauna-loyly.jpg` is by the HUUM account on Unsplash and shows a HUUM heater.
Its crop on this site is deliberately held low and tight on the stones and the
water stream so no competitor's product is legible. **Replace it first** when
Stovehaus has its own product photography.

The Shop page says out loud that its photographs are stock and not our floor,
because claiming otherwise would break voice rule 2 — every claim carries a
number or a name, or it gets cut.
